“How much does wedding videography cost?” is the first question we get, and it's completely understandable. A wedding budget has a lot of items, and each one has to fit somewhere. The honest answer is: it depends — but on a few clear things, and once you know them, it's easy to work out how much you actually need.

## Starting price and final amount

The starting prices of our packages are listed on the [wedding videography](route:servicesWeddings) page, so you can see straight away where we start. A starting price is, as the name says, a starting point. The final amount depends on your day — how long it lasts, where it takes place and what you want to end up with — and we agree on it after a short conversation.

That isn't a way of inflating the price, but of making sure you pay for what you actually need. A wedding that starts at noon and ends at midnight across three locations isn't the same as a registry office ceremony followed by lunch for thirty.

## Three packages, three levels

We put the packages together so that each one adds a new perspective to the film.

**Basic** is filming with a camera on the ground, with a gimbal for smooth movement and a wireless microphone on the vows. You get an edited film of the whole day, usually between one and three hours long. The exact length can't be predicted in advance, because it depends on the day itself — how long it lasts and how much happens — but we always aim to fill it with as much emotion as possible and with the moments you'll want to watch again. It's the package for couples who mainly want everything captured beautifully and cleanly.

**Standard** adds the drone. The church, the restaurant and the landscape from the air give the film breadth and a sense of place, and you also get a highlight spot of the best moments of the day, set to music, plus a vertical version for social media.

**Premium** adds the FPV drone: a flight through the empty hall before the guests arrive, in shots a classic camera can't make. In this package two videographers film all day, and a static camera records the whole church ceremony.

We deliberately don't list the package prices here, because they change — the current prices are always on the service page.

## What else affects the price

Apart from the package you choose, a few things change the final amount the most.

**Length of filming.** A day that starts with the couple getting ready in the morning and goes on late into the night takes more hours on location, but also more hours of editing. The longer the day, the more footage there is to review and shape into a story.

**Location.** Our base is Belgrade and the surrounding area. For weddings further away — elsewhere in Serbia or in the region — we agree terms separately, because travel then becomes part of the calculation.

**Extras.** Some things are part of the larger packages, and some can be added to any package by arrangement. The most common are:

- **a static camera in the church**, which films the whole ceremony without a break and captures the emotions of the guests and the couple from another angle;
- **a highlight spot**, a few minutes set to music, which you'll most often show to friends and share on social media.

## How to compare quotes

When you get several quotes, don't compare only the amounts. Compare what exactly the price includes: how many hours of filming, how many videographers, whether a drone and a highlight spot are included, and whether travel is charged separately. A quote that looks more expensive often turns out to be better value once all the items are added up.
